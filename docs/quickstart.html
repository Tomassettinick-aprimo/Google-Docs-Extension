🚀 Getting Started

You're three steps away from accessing your Aprimo DAM assets inside Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides.

1

Open the Add-on

In any Google Doc, Sheet, or Slide, click the 🔗 Aprimo DAM menu in the top menu bar, then click Open Aprimo DAM. The Aprimo sidebar will appear on the right side of your document.

2

Connect to your Aprimo tenant

When the sidebar loads, enter your Aprimo tenant name (the part before .aprimo.com in your DAM URL) and click Connect. You'll be redirected to your Aprimo login page to authorize the connection. Your credentials are stored securely in your own Google account — never on any external server.

3

Search and use your assets

Type any search term in the Search tab to find assets. Click an asset to preview it, then choose your action: Insert an image into your document, Open & Edit a Word/Excel/PowerPoint file in Google, Save to Drive, or Upload the current document back to Aprimo.

What you can do

🔍

Search & Insert

Search by keyword, file type, or asset ID. Insert images directly at your cursor in Google Docs or onto your Slides canvas.

✏️

Open & Edit

Pull a DOCX, XLSX, or PPTX from Aprimo into Google Drive. Edit it as a Google file, then save it back as a new version — without ever leaving Google Workspace.

⬆️

Upload the current document

Use the Upload tab (or the Aprimo DAM menu → Upload Current Document) to export and upload the active Google file to Aprimo as a new asset or new version.

🕐

Recent Assets

The Recent tab shows the last 10 assets you interacted with, with full action buttons — no need to search again.

💡 Tip: To disconnect from Aprimo or switch tenants, use 🔗 Aprimo DAM → Disconnect from Aprimo in the menu bar, then reconnect with your new tenant name.
