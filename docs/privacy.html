Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the Aprimo DAM for Google Workspace add-on ("the Add-on", "we", "our") handles information when you use it. This add-on is open source and released under the MIT License.

🔒 The short version: this Add-on does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to any server we operate. All credentials and settings are stored exclusively in your own Google account's user properties, and all asset data flows directly between your browser and your Aprimo tenant.

1. What data the Add-on accesses

To function, the Add-on reads and writes the following data:

  • Google Docs / Sheets / Slides content — the Add-on reads the active document's name and ID for upload, and inserts images you choose at your cursor. It only accesses the currently open file (*.currentonly scopes).
  • Google Drive files — when you use Open & Edit, the Add-on creates a temporary copy of an Office file in your Drive (using drive.file scope, which restricts access to files the Add-on itself creates). This file is deleted from Drive after you save the new version back to Aprimo.
  • Your Google account email — read once to namespace your per-user settings so multiple users on the same script deployment don't share credentials.
  • Your Aprimo API credentials — the OAuth 2.0 access token obtained from your Aprimo tenant is stored in Google Apps Script User Properties, which is scoped to your Google account and not readable by other users.

2. What data is NOT collected

  • No personal data is sent to any server operated by this project's maintainers.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking of any kind is implemented.
  • No document content is stored outside of your own Google account or your Aprimo tenant.
  • No cookies are set outside of Google's own authentication mechanisms.

3. How your Aprimo credentials are stored

When you connect to Aprimo, the Add-on completes an OAuth 2.0 PKCE flow with your Aprimo tenant. The resulting access token is stored in Google Apps Script User Properties — a key/value store built into Google that is:

  • Scoped to your individual Google account
  • Not accessible to other users of the same script
  • Stored entirely within Google's infrastructure
  • Deleted when you click "Disconnect from Aprimo" in the add-on

4. Data flows

All network requests made by the Add-on go to one of two destinations only:

  • Your Aprimo tenant (https://<your-tenant>.dam.aprimo.com and https://<your-tenant>.aprimo.com) — for asset search, download, upload, and version management.
  • Google APIs (googleapis.com) — for reading/writing Drive files and document content via your authorized scopes.

No data is routed through any proxy, analytics platform, or third-party service.

5. Open source & auditability

This Add-on is fully open source. Every line of code that runs in your browser and on Google's Apps Script servers is publicly available at:

https://github.com/Tomassettinick-aprimo/Google-Docs-Extension

You are encouraged to review the source code, report issues, or fork and self-host the Add-on under the terms of the MIT License.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

This Add-on's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Children's privacy

This Add-on is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this URL and the "Last updated" date at the top will be revised. Because the Add-on is open source, all changes are also visible in the GitHub commit history.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or the Add-on can be raised by opening an issue on GitHub: github.com/Tomassettinick-aprimo/Google-Docs-Extension/issues
